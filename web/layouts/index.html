03 / The question underneath

What does rendering
teach us about inference?

A renderer has more possible light paths than it can afford to trace. An inference engine has more intermediate data than it wants to move through memory. Both make you think carefully about what to keep, what to combine, and when to reuse earlier work.

Those are the connections I’m here to explore. ReSTIR’s reservoirs carry a small amount of running state; attention kernels also combine partial results without keeping every intermediate. The operations differ, but the questions about memory, scheduling, and GPU occupancy are familiar.

Serenity gives me a way to investigate them with something visible at the end. For now, the path tracer is built. ReSTIR is the next piece, in progress on a branch.
