The milestones record what I built, what I measured, and what changed my mind along the way. Milestone 1 built the baseline. Milestone 2 follows the work into ReSTIR, path reuse, and light focused through glass. Milestone 3 builds and trains a model to turn those samples into a cleaner, larger image, then brings its inference into the renderer. Milestone 4 makes that frame fast, with the optimizations shipped engines use and a network retrained for what they changed. Milestone 5 moves the frame to an NVIDIA GPU and arranges its work for that hardware. Milestone 6 does the same for Apple silicon, and moves the network to the Neural Engine.
