# Manifold next event estimation: caustics through glass

*2026-10-12. Apple M3 Max, macOS 26.6.2, Xcode 26.2, release builds,
`scenes/marbles.toml`. Base is 15471ad (ReSTIR PT Phase 2); built by the
user's direction. Production reference: Blender Cycles' MNEE ("shadow
caustics", `intern/cycles/kernel/integrator/mnee.h`, read 2026-10-11) and
Hanika, Droske and Fascione 2015.*

## What MNEE covers here, and what it does not

Cycles' domain, unchanged: light reaching a rough receiver through 1 to 4
refractions at smooth casters, no reflection among them, seeded by the
straight segment from the receiver to a light point drawn as next event
estimation draws it, so inside the casters' shadow of that point.

- **Covered:** caustics on the table under clear and tinted glass marbles
  (two crossings a marble), the tint's absorption along the chain, and the
  cat's-eye cores lit through their clear shell (one crossing).
- **Not covered:** caustics reflected off the metal marbles (no refraction),
  and fireflies seen directly through glass or in mirrors (no rough vertex:
  that is the pixel's sampling, not its lighting).

Receivers and casters are derived from materials, as the scene format has no
flag: a receiver is a surface with a diffuse lobe (rough, and the coated
base), a caster a dielectric sphere (smooth by construction; a dielectric
box is refused, as Cycles refuses a caster without smooth normals).

## The split, before MNEE (step 1)

How much of the path tracer's error at equal time comes from each kind of
path, in the glass and metal mask Phase 1 used and over the whole frame. A
diagnostic build (not committed) ran the path loop with a gather that kept
one category's light at a time, the same numbers drawn in every run (the
categories' images sum to the whole to 3e-7):

- (a) a rough bounce, then only refractions (1 or more) to the light:
  MNEE's domain;
- (b) no rough bounce before the light: a firefly seen through glass or in a
  mirror from the camera, or directly;
- (c) a rough bounce and a metal bounce since it: caustics reflected off
  metal;
- (d) everything else (direct light at rough surfaces, its interreflection).

Each category's share is the fraction of the k-frame image's error (k = 4,
1920 x 1080, as Phase 2's equal-time comparison; mean of 8 windows of 4
frames) that would go if that category alone were noiseless: its 4-frame
part replaced by a 512-frame mean. Against the 65536-sample references.
Shares need not sum to 1 (the categories' errors are correlated within a
path, and the clamp is not linear).

| share of the error | t = 120 MSE | t = 120 clamped | t = 8 MSE | t = 8 clamped |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| glass+metal: (a) through glass onto rough (MNEE's) | 0.247 | 0.142 | 0.149 | 0.220 |
| glass+metal: (b) specular chain from the camera | 0.733 | 0.577 | 0.778 | 0.651 |
| glass+metal: (c) reflected off metal | 0.003 | 0.004 | 0.002 | 0.006 |
| glass+metal: (d) other | 0.009 | 0.082 | 0.052 | 0.140 |
| whole: (a) | 0.055 | 0.068 | 0.018 | 0.077 |
| whole: (b) | 0.924 | 0.458 | 0.858 | 0.445 |
| whole: (c) | 0.001 | 0.005 | 0.000 | 0.007 |
| whole: (d) | 0.010 | 0.395 | 0.091 | 0.496 |

So in the glass and metal mask MNEE's domain is 15 to 25% of the error at
equal time; three quarters is (b), fireflies seen through glass and in
mirrors, which no lighting estimator reaches; reflected caustics are under
1%.

## What was built

- **MNEE** (`src/metal/integrator/mnee.metal.h`, whose header states the
  algorithm and every departure): the seed, one ray query along the
  segment collecting caster-sphere crossings (at most 4); Cycles' Newton
  walk on the half-vector constraints, its block-tridiagonal solve, step
  control and constants; the generalized geometry term from Cycles'
  transfer matrix; Fresnel and the radiance's eta^2 at each crossing;
  absorption over every segment; visibility of each segment. Departures,
  each stated in the header: no clamp on G (Cycles clamps at 2 in meters,
  which at marble scale would remove nearly all of every caustic);
  Cycles' absolute distances made relative to the caster's radius;
  projection onto a sphere by its roots, not a traced ray; the progress
  test over the whole chain, not each crossing; each crossing's tangent
  frame built from the seed's tangent (an orthonormal basis rebuilt from n
  at each point had a Jacobian ~10% off, found against finite differences,
  which confined Newton to seeds near their solution).
- **No double counting**: a light a BSDF ray strikes after a receiver's
  diffuse bounce and 1 to 4 refractions at caster spheres is not counted
  (`mnee::covered()`), in the path tracer, in ReSTIR PT's tree and in its
  shifts' replay; reconnection keeps the source's chain. Narrower than
  Cycles' cull (any light after a caster after a receiver): exactly MNEE's
  domain.
- **A work list** (contract 16, `core/contracts/mnee_request.h`; the MNEE
  pass, `metal/passes/mnee/`; storage `metal/frame/mnee_list.h`). Solved
  inside the path loop, MNEE made the path pass 124 ms at 3456 x 2234
  (path alone 30.4): a tenth of the paths solve, and nearly every SIMD group
  of 32 waits on one. As Cycles moved MNEE into its own kernel, the loop
  now writes a 128-byte request where the seed crosses casters, and the
  MNEE pass, recorded right after the loop's dispatch, solves the requests
  one thread each and adds each one's light to its pixel (atomic float
  adds); the path pass folds it into the accumulated image, ReSTIR PT's
  initial pass into the light buffer. 124 -> 59 ms. Capacity one request a
  pixel; an overflow makes the path's sample not finite (counted).
- **ReSTIR PT**: MNEE's paths out of ReSTIR PT's path space, estimated per
  pixel each frame and not reused: NEE's kept RIS candidate's segment is
  the seed; crossing casters, a request weighed by throughput over
  survival times W_RIS, no candidate offered. The tree with MNEE is a
  kernel of its own (`restir_pt_initial_mnee`), so without MNEE the tree
  compiles as before.
- **The setting**: top-level `mnee = true` in a graph with a path or a
  ReSTIR PT pass (`core/frame/schedule.h`; refused elsewhere, and if not a
  bool). Two path kernels (`path_trace`, `path_trace_mnee`).

## The default

The rule, fixed before the run: `graphs/restir_pt.toml` sets `mnee = true`
if ReSTIR PT + MNEE has lower displayed MSE than ReSTIR PT alone, both
against the path tracer at their own equal-time k, in the glass and metal
region at both times. It does, by either reading (the MSE itself: 0.0309 <
0.0370, 0.0450 < 0.0472; the ratio to the path tracer: 1.31 > 1.12, 1.01 >
0.99). So it is on. `graphs/path.toml` stays without MNEE, the reference.
