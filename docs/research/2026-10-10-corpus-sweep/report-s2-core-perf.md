# Performance guidelines review of `src/core/` (branch `marbles`)

I read all 64 files in `src/core/`. I walked every rule in the 10 categories, and checked the SIMD category (8 rules) as well. I used `get_guideline` on every rule whose fit was unclear and on every perf ID the code's comments cite. I made no edits, builds or commits. Nothing from `sweep1-findings.md` is repeated here.

The `cpp-perf-guidelines` MCP server failed to connect (ECONNREFUSED). The `perf.py` route to localhost:7015 worked, and every lookup went through it.

**Overall:**
- **Per-frame core path is clean.** `animate`, `glow`, `position`, `world_bounds`, `History::join`, `FrameTimes` and `shader_form` allocate nothing and throw nothing on success. Its only costs are how the lookups are laid out (rows 5 and 6) and a re-check each frame (row 19).
- **Load is the dominant cost.** It is driven by how the still shapes are tested for distance (rows 1–3), and its total work has no cap (row 3).

## Perf IDs cited in code comments

All 16 citations say what the comment claims; I found no mismatch.

- **MEM.9** (animate.h:52, flight.h:137): no steady-state allocation; confirmed, nothing allocated per frame.
- **CONC.1, CONC.3** (flight.cpp:550-551): a relaxed job counter with no ordering obligation, and no contention at one increment per flight.
- **CACHE.1** (flight.cpp:553): the rule itself says to measure first and not to pad data written rarely; the decision not to pad is consistent.
- **CACHE.5** (frame_constants.h:17, surface_interaction.h:43): fields are in alignment order with no internal padding.
- **GPU.3, GPU.10** (surface_interaction.h:46): treating register pressure as judged by measured frame time matches both rules.
- **GPU.10, TLM.11** (frame_times.h:17-19): GPU time is taken as a difference of two host-time instants, so no CPU/GPU clock alignment is needed.
- **GDSA.3** (swarm.h:34, noise.h:25, swirl.h:44): numbers are keyed by (seed, stream, counter).
- **GPU.4** (camera.h:52, wood.h:81): the lens branch is uniform across a dispatch, and the seam branch is coherent between neighbouring pixels.
